Montrose Beach at a Glance

Feature Details
Beach Hours 6:00 AM – 11:00 PM daily
Bird Sanctuary Hours Dawn to dusk daily
Bird Species Recorded 300+
Best Birdwatching Season Spring & fall migration
Parking Free on Montrose Ave & Simonds Dr; paid lot near harbor
Nearby Dining The Dock at Montrose Beach

Things to Do at Montrose Beach

  • Swim or relax on the sand
  • Walk the harbor breakwater
  • Birdwatch at the Magic Hedge
  • Bike or jog along the lakefront trail
  • Let your dog play at Montrose Dog Beach
  • Grab food and live music at The Dock
